## About the Project

This project was born out of a personal need to visually map out my favorite songs and artists. 

Categorizing a track isn't always as simple as labeling it "rock" or "jazz", they often belong to niche subgenres and at times, they blend influences from completely different musical styles. 

PlaylistSampler allows you to analyze your playlists by uncovering detailed data insights and generating custom graphs to visualize your favorite tracks and their connections.
